A schematic mammalian complex. The bilayer is cut open; the head cutaway removes one front α subunit and part of a β subunit to expose γ. Rounded domains, pocket openings and nucleotide symbols are illustrative, not atomic coordinates. The simplified open/loose/tight cycle omits molecular substates. Counts show ideal coupling in a primed synthesis cycle; timing is slowed, substrates are available, and ADP/phosphate transport costs are excluded. Separating parts pauses the mechanism.
