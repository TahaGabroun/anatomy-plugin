Experimental protein geometry; illustrative catalytic motion. The supplied 8H9S-labelled assembly places the α₃β₃ head, central stalk, peripheral stalk, subunit a, and c₈ ring in their experimentally resolved relative positions. In this teaching animation, the c-ring and central stalk rotate together while the head stays stationary. The β-subunit movement is a small illustrative deformation of the deposited state-1 coordinates. Evidence & limits
The default protein view is built from heavy-atom coordinates in the supplied human cryo-EM assembly PDB 8H9S (reported 2.53 Å). The supplied assembly file identifies its entry as XXXX, so the coordinate-file identity is not independently authenticated against the archive. Spheres are a subsampled, atom-centred rendering, not a calculated solvent-accessible surface. The ring/shaft motion, β-domain deformation, proton tracks and synthesis counters remain pedagogical illustrations, not experimental trajectories or molecular dynamics. The machine view is an analogy, not molecular geometry.
